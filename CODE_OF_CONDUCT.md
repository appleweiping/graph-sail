# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the technical substance of a disagreement.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Keep reviews focused on code and observable behavior rather than people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, sexualized attention, insults, threats, stalking, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary. The privacy
and safety of reporters will be respected.

This policy applies in repositories, issue trackers, review threads, community spaces, and public
events where someone is representing the project.
